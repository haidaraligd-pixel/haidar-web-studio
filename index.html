Questions

The things people ask me first

How long does a website take?

Three days for a single-page site, about a week for a five-page business site. If you need it faster, tell me — rush delivery is possible for a small extra fee.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, a logo if you have one, any photos, and the text you want on the site. If you do not have text ready, I will draft it and you can correct it — most clients find that easier.

Can I edit the website myself afterwards?

Yes. If you want to change things yourself, we build on WordPress and you get a short training call. If you would rather I handle it, that is what the monthly care plan is for.

What if I do not like the design?

You see a preview before you pay the balance, and every package includes revision rounds. If the first direction is wrong, we change it — that is normal and expected, not a problem.

How do I pay from Pakistan?

JazzCash, Easypaisa or direct bank transfer. Half to start, half when the site is ready. International clients can pay through PayPal.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes — most of my clients are abroad. I work across time zones and I am happy to do calls outside your normal working hours to make the timing work.
